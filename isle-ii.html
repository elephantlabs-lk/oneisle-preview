01 Welcome to One Isle II

A new landmark
in Wijerama.

Modern living meets convenience, all in one perfect location.

Welcome to One Isle II, an exciting new development that seamlessly combines modern living with premium commercial opportunities. Nestled in the thriving yet peaceful area of Wijerama, it offers a unique blend of convenience and luxury for residents and businesses alike.

With a carefully designed mix of residential apartments and commercial spaces, One Isle II is for those looking for a home that offers more than a place to live — it's a lifestyle. From state-of-the-art amenities to eco-friendly sustainability initiatives, it provides everything you need.
