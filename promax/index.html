We build more than just houses. We create homes filled with warmth, comfort and a sense of belonging — a serene haven where thoughtful design fosters joy, and where every resident feels truly at home.

(04) The One Isle standard

Designed around the way you live.

  • Every corner is meticulously designed to balance luxury and practicality — where elegance meets functionality in every detail.

  • High-end finishes, premium materials and modern fittings — from sleek, fully equipped kitchens with custom pantry cupboards to serene, air-conditioned master bedrooms.

  • A scenic rooftop, modern gymnasium, dedicated parking, advanced CCTV security, 24/7 backup power and a Fingara Club lifetime membership.

  • Solar energy, rainwater harvesting, eco-conscious car washing — and a 20-year structural warranty on every development.

  • A cool, calm corner of Nugegoda — moments from the University of Sri Jayewardenepura, supermarkets, healthcare, dining and Fingara Club.
