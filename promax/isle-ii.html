(01) Welcome

A new landmark in Wijerama.

Modern living meets convenience, all in one perfect location.

Welcome to One Isle II, a development that seamlessly combines modern living with premium commercial opportunities. Nestled in the thriving yet peaceful area of Wijerama, it offers a unique blend of convenience and luxury for residents and businesses alike.

With a carefully designed mix of residential apartments and commercial spaces, One Isle II is for those looking for a home that offers more than a place to live — it's a lifestyle, from state-of-the-art amenities to eco-friendly sustainability initiatives.
