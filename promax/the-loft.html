(01) Welcome

Luxury living, quietly placed.

More than just a place to live — a place to thrive.

Each apartment at One Isle Loft is equipped with high-end finishes, fully equipped kitchens and private balconies. With 24/7 security, backup power and exclusive amenities — a gymnasium, rooftop and parking — it offers everything for a modern, comfortable lifestyle.

Located in a serene neighbourhood of Nugegoda, close to the University of Sri Jayewardenepura, shopping, dining and entertainment.
