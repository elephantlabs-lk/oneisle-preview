About One Isle

The One Isle blueprint.

Nugegoda, Sri LankaEst. One Isle Residencies

(01) Who we are

Welcome to One Isle Residencies — where your house becomes a home, filled with comfort, joy and a deep sense of belonging. We transform houses into homes by providing a nurturing haven where you feel safe, valued and truly at home.

Vision

To be the benchmark of modern urban living in Sri Lanka, creating homes that foster community, comfort and sustainable living.

Mission

To craft thoughtfully designed living spaces that blend luxury with functionality, prioritise safety and sustainability, and nurture a vibrant community — providing residents across Sri Lanka with a serene, secure and connected environment where they can build lasting memories.
