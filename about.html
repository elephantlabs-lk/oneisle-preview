01 Who we are

Where a house
becomes a home.

Homes filled with comfort, joy and a deep sense of belonging.

At One Isle Residencies, we believe in creating more than just living spaces — we craft homes filled with warmth and belonging. Our community is built for comfort and connection.

Here, a serene ambience and thoughtful design foster joy and relaxation. We transform houses into homes by providing a nurturing haven where you feel safe, valued and truly at home.

“

Owning a home is a keystone of wealth — both financial affluence and emotional security.

Avishka Thushan PadmasiriDirector, One Isle Residencies

02 Vision

The benchmark of modern urban living.

To be the benchmark of modern urban living in Sri Lanka, creating homes that foster community, comfort and sustainable living, where every resident feels truly at home.

03 Mission

Thoughtful spaces, lasting memories.

To craft thoughtfully designed living spaces that blend luxury with functionality, prioritise safety and sustainability, and nurture a vibrant community — providing residents across Sri Lanka with a serene, secure and connected environment where they can build lasting memories and enjoy a fulfilling lifestyle.

“

Our vision for One Isle Residencies was to create a space that embodies sustainable living, affordable luxury and unparalleled comfort — all within a serene environment where residents can truly feel at home.

Mahesha D LiyanageOne Isle Architect · BSc (BE), MSc (Arch), AIA SL
